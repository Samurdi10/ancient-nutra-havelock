Privacy Policy — Havelock Orders

Last updated: 2026

Havelock Orders is an internal business application used by staff of Ancient Nutra (Havelock City Mall) to record daily sales, manage physical stock counts and purchase orders, and — for authorized staff only — connect to QuickBooks Online for accounting and inventory purposes. It is not a public consumer product and is not available for sign-up by the general public.

What information we collect

We collect and store: staff account information (name/email) used for sign-in, sales and stock records entered or uploaded by staff (bills, stock counts, purchase orders), and, if a staff member connects QuickBooks Online, the OAuth access and refresh tokens needed to communicate with the connected QuickBooks Online company on the business's behalf.

How we use this information

This information is used solely to operate the Havelock Orders application for its intended business purpose: tracking sales and stock for the Havelock City Mall outlet, and, where connected, syncing that data to QuickBooks Online as Sales Receipts, Inventory Adjustments, and Bills.

How we share this information

We do not sell or share this information with third parties, except as necessary to operate the integration itself — i.e. sending data to Intuit's QuickBooks Online API, at the direction of authorized staff who have connected their QuickBooks Online account.

Data storage

Application data is stored using Supabase (a hosted PostgreSQL database provider). QuickBooks OAuth tokens are stored server-side and are never exposed to the browser.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be directed to the Ancient Nutra business administrator.
